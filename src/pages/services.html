In-Home Dementia Care

Personalized support, built around the person

Every Florida family's situation is different. Our in-home dementia care services are flexible and designed to grow with your needs — from occasional dementia companionship to full daily in-home support and live-in care. Everything centres on the person, not the task.

01
Companionship

Companionship Visits

Loneliness and isolation are two of the most significant challenges for people living with dementia. Our companions visit regularly, building a trusted friendship over time — a relationship in which your loved one is known, understood, and genuinely looked forward to seeing.

Visits are built around the individual. The companion learns what lifts your loved one's spirits: which songs bring a smile, what topics spark stories, when to be lively and when to simply sit quietly together.

  • A consistent companion — the same face every visit
  • Conversation, music, and activities your loved one genuinely enjoys
  • Gentle reminiscence and memory engagement
  • Visits tailored to mood and energy on the day
  • A brief handover note shared after every visit

02
Daily Living

In-Home Support

Daily life at home can become gradually more challenging as dementia progresses. Our companions help with gentle reminders, light food preparation, accompanying your loved one on local errands, and supporting daily routines in a calm, familiar way.

We work to an agreed plan that reflects your loved one's existing routine — not one we impose. Small details matter: the order of the morning, the preferred cup, the way the day is structured. This is practical help delivered with humanity.

  • Gentle reminders for meals and medication
  • Light food preparation and kitchen support
  • Accompanying your loved one on local errands
  • Supporting morning or evening routines
  • Maintaining independence, not replacing it

Caregiver Respite

Florida family caregivers are some of the most dedicated people we know — and some of the most exhausted. Caregiver respite for dementia is not a luxury: it is essential to sustaining your own health, your relationships, and your capacity to continue caring over the long term.

When a companion arrives for a respite session, they take over fully and attentively — not as a babysitter, but as a skilled, caring presence who knows your loved one. Families consistently tell us that starting respite was the moment things felt sustainable for the first time.

  • Regular fixed slots or flexible as-needed cover

  • You are reachable by phone at all times if needed

Meaningful Activity

Memory & Engagement Activities

Meaningful activity is one of the most powerful tools in dementia care — but meaningful is the operative word. Activities chosen for the person, not just for the diagnosis. Our companions draw on a person's lifetime of interests, skills, and identity.

Reminiscence is a particularly powerful part of this service. Thoughtful conversation about the past can produce remarkable moments of clarity and joy. Companions are trained in reminiscence techniques and know how to follow a person's lead.

  • Music — listening, singing, sharing familiar melodies
  • Gardening, cookery, and gentle crafts
  • Looking through photographs and reminiscing
  • Reading aloud or listening to favourite books
  • Trained reminiscence techniques, not a generic activity list

What to Expect

What a typical visit looks like

No two visits are identical — every person and every day is different. But here is a sense of the shape a companionship visit takes.

1

A familiar arrival

The companion arrives at a consistent time with the same warm greeting. Familiar rituals — a cup of tea, a favourite chair — signal this is a safe, known interaction.

2

Following the person's lead

The companion reads the person's energy and mood — conversation or quiet company, lively or gentle. The care plan is a guide, not a script.

3

Activity or engagement

A photograph album, tending the garden, a game of cards, or simply a long conversation — engagement is kept meaningful and unhurried.

4

A warm handover note

At the end of each visit, the companion completes a brief note — how the person seemed, what they enjoyed, any observations worth sharing.

Flexible Scheduling

Scheduling that works around your life

We know that care needs rarely follow a neat, predictable pattern. Visits can be arranged as frequently or as flexibly as your family requires — and adjusted easily as your needs change over time.

We build visits around your existing routine, not ours. If mornings are better, or certain days are especially important, we build that in from the start. There is no minimum contract and no rigid package. The right schedule is whatever genuinely helps your family, and we will work that out together.

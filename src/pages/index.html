Our Services

Companionship Visits

Loneliness and isolation are two of the most significant challenges for people living with dementia. Our companions visit regularly, building a trusted friendship over time — a relationship in which your loved one is known, understood, and genuinely looked forward to seeing.

  • A consistent companion — the same face every visit
  • Conversation, music, and activities your loved one genuinely enjoys
  • Gentle reminiscence and memory engagement
  • Visits tailored to mood and energy on the day
  • A brief handover note shared after every visit

In-Home Support

Daily life at home can become gradually more challenging as dementia progresses. Our companions help with gentle reminders, light food preparation, accompanying your loved one on local errands, and supporting daily routines in a calm, familiar way — maintaining independence rather than replacing it.

  • Gentle reminders for meals and medication
  • Light food preparation and kitchen support
  • Accompanying your loved one on local errands
  • Supporting morning or evening routines
  • Maintaining independence, not replacing it

Caregiver Respite

Florida family caregivers are some of the most dedicated people we know — and some of the most exhausted. When a companion arrives for a respite session, they take over fully and attentively — not as a babysitter, but as a skilled, caring presence who knows your loved one. You have genuine time away without the constant background hum of worry.

  • The same companion your loved one already knows and trusts
  • Regular fixed slots or flexible as-needed cover
  • No babysitter — a trained, caring professional
  • You are reachable by phone at all times if needed
  • Genuine peace of mind, not just time away

Memory & Engagement Activities

Meaningful activity is one of the most powerful tools in dementia care — but meaningful is the operative word. Activities chosen for the person, not just for the diagnosis. Our companions draw on a person's lifetime of interests, skills, and identity, and are trained in reminiscence techniques that can produce remarkable moments of clarity and joy.

  • Music — listening, singing, sharing familiar melodies
  • Gardening, cookery, and gentle crafts
  • Looking through photographs and reminiscing
  • Reading aloud or listening to favourite books
  • Trained reminiscence techniques, not a generic activity list

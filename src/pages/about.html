Meet Our Founders

Built from personal experience

Dementia Companions was founded by two people who had experienced dementia care from the inside — a nurse, and a family member who had cared for a parent with dementia. Both had seen first-hand what good care could look like, and what it so often did not: agency staff who changed constantly, strangers who didn't know the family's routines, and a system built around availability rather than compatibility.

Together, they founded Dementia Companions to fix that. Every companion is hand-selected, dementia-trained, and matched to the specific personality, interests, and routines of your loved one — then they stay. The same face, every visit.

What sets us apart isn't just the training — it's the commitment. We don't shuffle companions based on scheduling. When we make a match, we protect it. That consistency is what allows real trust to form between your loved one and the person showing up at their door.

What We Stand For

Our values shape every match we make

We believe the quality of a companion relationship shapes the entire care experience for your loved one. These three principles guide every match we make — and every decision we take on behalf of the families we serve.

01

Consistency above all

Dementia care is built on familiarity. We match once and we keep that relationship — the same companion, the same routines, visit after visit. No strangers, no surprises. Familiar faces reduce anxiety and help your loved one feel genuinely at home, even on harder days.

02

Personality-first matching

We don't assign whoever is available. We interview families and companions to find a genuine personality fit — because a companion your loved one actually looks forward to seeing is a companion who can truly help.

03

Trained specifically for dementia

Every companion passes Florida's Level 2 AHCA background screening before training even begins — covering dementia communication, de-escalation, safeguarding, personal care, and emergency procedures.

Simple Process

How It Works

We've made starting as easy as possible. Your first call is free, informal, and completely without obligation.

1. Book a free call

A warm, no-pressure conversation to understand your situation and what you need — with no jargon and no commitment.

2. We match your companion

Carefully selected based on your loved one's personality, interests, and needs — typically within 48 hours of your call.

3. Regular care begins

Consistent visits, a real bond forming over time, and care that adapts as your loved one's needs evolve.

4. Updates after every visit

A brief handover note on mood, activities, and anything worth knowing — so you always know how your loved one is doing.

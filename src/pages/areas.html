Outside Our Current Coverage?

We can still help — wherever you are in Florida

If we do not yet serve your area, we are sorry — but we do not want you to leave empty-handed. We can suggest alternative resources, specialist organisations, and care providers who may be able to help.

Our resources also include a comprehensive guide to dementia support organisations, legal and financial planning help, and practical guidance for families — regardless of where you live.

Get in touch anyway
  • A free conversation about your situation and options
  • Signposting to reputable providers in your area
  • Guidance from our resources library (free, always)
  • Notification when we expand to your area
  • Referral to the Alzheimer's Association helpline
Good to Know

How our coverage works

Flexible boundaries

Our area listings represent where the majority of our companions are based — not hard borders. If you are just outside a listed area, please get in touch. We cover many nearby locations not explicitly listed and will always check before saying no.

Local companions

All our companions live locally within the areas they serve. This means they know the neighbourhoods, understand local resources, and can be consistent — arriving reliably without long commute times that affect availability.

Expanding regularly

We are growing steadily and adding new areas every few months. Fort Lauderdale and Sarasota/Naples are next. If your area is not listed, registering your interest helps us prioritise where we expand — your message directly influences our decisions.

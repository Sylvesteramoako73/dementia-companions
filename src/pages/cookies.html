What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better experience by remembering your preferences and understanding how you use our site.

How We Use Cookies

Dementia Companions uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function properly, such as maintaining your session and security settings.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website so we can improve the experience. We may use tools such as Google Analytics for this purpose.
  • Functional cookies: Remember your preferences and settings to enhance your visit.

Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete existing ones. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

To find out more about managing cookies in your specific browser, please visit your browser's help documentation.

Third-Party Cookies

Some pages on our site may include content from third-party services (such as embedded maps or contact form providers) that may set their own cookies. We do not control these cookies and recommend reviewing the privacy policies of those services.

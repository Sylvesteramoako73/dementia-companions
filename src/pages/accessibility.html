Last updated: June 14, 2026

Our Commitment

Dementia Companions is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to all visitors, including people with disabilities. We strive to meet or exceed the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards.

Accessibility Features

We have taken the following steps to make our website more accessible:

  • Use of semantic HTML to support screen readers and assistive technologies
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and background elements
  • Descriptive alt text for images
  • Keyboard-navigable menus and interactive elements
  • Clear and consistent page structure with proper heading hierarchy
  • Readable fonts and appropriate font sizes

Known Limitations

While we work hard to make our entire website accessible, some older content or third-party tools may not fully meet accessibility standards. We are actively working to address any known issues.

Feedback and Contact

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of our website. If you experience any barriers or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us:

Email: support@dementiacompanions.com
We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within 5 business days.

Formal Complaints

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the U.S. Department of Justice Civil Rights Division or your state's disability rights organization for additional assistance.

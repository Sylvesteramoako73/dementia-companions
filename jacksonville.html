Serving Jacksonville

Local companions who know Jacksonville

Our Jacksonville companions live and work locally — they understand the neighborhoods, know the area, and can be relied upon to arrive consistently and on time. That local knowledge matters: familiar surroundings and a familiar face are among the most reassuring things we can offer someone living with dementia.

We serve families across Duval County, providing the same high standard of care and the same commitment to consistency in every home we visit. Whether you are in the center of Jacksonville or in a quieter neighborhood on the edge of the county, our team can reach you.

Getting started is simple: reach out, tell us about your loved one, and we will take it from there. The first conversation is free and without obligation.

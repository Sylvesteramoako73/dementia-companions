Questions families ask us

Can't find what you're looking for? Ask us directly.

A Dementia Companion is a trained, compassionate person who provides regular companionship and engagement to someone living with dementia. Unlike clinical carers, Companions focus on meaningful connection, activities, and emotional support.

A home carer focuses on physical tasks like personal hygiene and medication. A Dementia Companion fills a different role: genuine human connection, stimulating conversation, cognitive activities, and emotional support. Many families use both.

Common signs include increased isolation, mood changes, safety concerns when left alone, caregiver burnout in family members, or a decline in day-to-day engagement and routine.
